# Original project context

The supplied ENGDATA301 assignment asked for DQN and DDQN on CartPole and generalized DDQN on LunarLander, with running-average rewards measured over 100 episodes. Thresholds specified in the brief were 200 for solving and -75 for partial LunarLander success. Lecture 13A examples were allowed as a starting point.

The two supplied lecture notebooks are retained under `reference/` as teaching material, not presented as original project results. No author/license statement for the teaching material was supplied; its redistribution rights have not been established.
